About the data

Eviction filings come from the NY State Unified Court System's deidentified landlord-tenant extract for city and district courts. It covers seven city courts: Albany, Cohoes and Watervliet (Albany County), Troy and Rensselaer (Rensselaer County), Mechanicville (Saratoga County), and Schenectady. Saratoga Springs City Court is not in the current extract, so in Saratoga County only the Mechanicville ZIP code has data. Town and village courts are not in the extract, so suburban and rural ZIP codes appear as no data, not as places without evictions. ZIP codes that straddle a city line are partly undercounted for the same reason. Commercial cases are excluded. A filing is counted in the month it was filed and in the ZIP of the property address.

Rates are annualized: filings in the selected window, divided by the number of months, times 12, per 1,000 renter households. Tiers split ZIP codes with coverage into quartiles of the 2022 to latest-month rate, so the same color means the same rate whichever window you select.

Renter profile figures are from the American Community Survey 5-year estimates for ZIP Code Tabulation Areas. Income needed to afford rent is median gross rent × 12 ÷ 0.30. Rent-burden shares exclude households for which the ratio is not computed. Race and ethnicity describe the householder of renter-occupied units; Hispanic householders may be of any race, and "White" means White alone, not Hispanic.
